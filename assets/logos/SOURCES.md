# Logos

All logos in `brand/` are the official UGent, imec, IDLab and AIRO marks supplied by the lab, downsized for the web; light and dark variants per theme. Palette (AIRO colour sheet): slate 434A52, teal 7DB5A8, sand D6AE72, red B75659, blue 6595BF. The UGent mark links to ugent.be as its usage rules require.
